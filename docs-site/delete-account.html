Delete your account

Cupping - Coffee Rater

In the app (instant)

  1. Open the app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Account and password, then Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address you signed up with at williamawrigley@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, display name and sign-in.
  • All your reviews, including their ratings, tags, comments and photos.
  • Your saved cafes, the people you blocked, and any reports you made.

Cafe scores update without your reviews. Deleted data cannot be recovered. Backups made by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Only want to remove a review?

Go to Profile, then My reviews, tap the “⋮” on the review and choose Delete review. This removes just that review and its photo.
