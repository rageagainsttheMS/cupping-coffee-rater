Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Cupping - Coffee Rater (“the app”, “we”) lets people rate cafes on coffee, food, vibe and how good they are to work from. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can remove it. It is written for people in Australia and follows the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (or, if you use Google or Apple to sign in, the identifier they give us), and the display name you choose.
  • What you post: your ratings, tags, comments, the drinks you had, prices, beans, and any photo you add to a review. Reviews show your display name to other people.
  • Your saved cafes and the people you have blocked or reports you have made.
  • Your location, only if you allow it. We use it to centre the map, suggest the nearest cafe when you write a review, and to mark a review as a “verified visit” when you are within about 100 metres of the cafe. When you post, your position is checked against the cafe and then discarded. We do not store your coordinates and we do not track your movements.
  • Your settings on your phone, such as your theme and any review draft you have not posted. These stay on your phone.

Cafe information from Google

Cafe names, addresses, positions, opening hours and Google ratings come from Google Maps Platform. Searches you make for cafes are sent to Google. Google rating and opening hours are shown live and are not stored by us. The map is provided by Google Maps and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show the map, work out cafe scores, and show your reviews.
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports, hide content that breaks the Terms, and act on blocks.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Google provides maps, cafe data and, if you choose it, Google sign-in.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, if you choose it.

These providers may store data outside Australia. We only share what is needed for them to do their job.

What other people can see

Your display name, ratings, comments, photos and verified-visit badge on a review are visible to other signed-in users. Your email address is never shown to other users.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete a single review any time from Profile, then My reviews. You can delete your whole account from Profile, then Account and password, then Delete account. That removes your profile, reviews, photos, saved cafes, blocks and reports. See how to delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Write to us at the address below. You can also complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

The app is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
